# The Daily World — 23 November 2026

A one-image newspaper front page summing up the world news of Monday
23 November 2026, drawn programmatically by `main.py` (pure Python
standard library, deterministic, no network).

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Sum up 23 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123).

## The stories on the page

1. **Nvidia** reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO after President Trump
   intervened; shares leapt 31% and the Nasdaq jumped about 7%
   (Reuters, 23 Nov 2026).
2. **COP31** in Antalya, Turkiye ended with no deal after talks
   collapsed over fossil-fuel wording; the EU rejected the pact
   (CNN / Reuters, reported 23 Nov 2026).
3. **Ukraine**: President Zelensky confirmed seven Ukrainian sea
   drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, Libya
   (CNN / WSJ, 23 Nov 2026).
4. **Indonesia**: the death toll from the Cikande, Banten candy
   factory fire was confirmed at 24 on Monday, with about 30 people
   still missing (Reuters, 23 Nov 2026).
5. **FIFA**: England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales were
   confirmed as hosts of the Women's World Cup 2035; the USA-led
   group hosts 2031 (FIFA / Reuters, 23 Nov 2026).
6. Footer ticker: CrowdStrike to acquire Zscaler in a $43.9 billion
   deal (Reuters, 23 Nov 2026).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
# writes out/entry.png (1024x1024 PNG, well under 1 MiB)
```
